01 · Creative direction

An engineering journal, printed with editorial discipline.

The record, made legible

Everything is drawn structure: rails, seams, squares, chips. No decoration, no dashboards — the artifact itself is the imagery, so the page shows the product instead of illustrating it.

Calm and accountable

Near-mono ink on a warm paper ground, one red, generous air, flush-left everything. Trust reads as restraint: nothing glows, nothing floats, every claim carries a receipt.

The human is the accent

Red is budgeted for human moments: the primary action, the lesson node, the review annotation, one poster close. AI drafts render quiet and dashed; confirmation renders solid.

A note on the brief: the requested five-hue semantic set (blue, violet, amber, green, red) is collapsed into the bound mono system. Semantic state is carried by mark, weight and label — solid, outline, dashed — so meaning never rides on hue at all, which is stricter than the accessibility bar the brief sets. The dark-layered mood is delivered as two ink fields and a red field on the paper ground rather than a dark page.

05 · Spacing & layout
1200px

Content column, generous clamp gutters. Full-bleed only for the three fields (two ink, one red).

4px base

Token scale for components; sections breathe at 72px, panels at 20–28px. Air replaces boxes wherever possible.

2px seams

Sections are separated by drawn rules, never by background changes. Internal structure uses 1px; hierarchy is rule weight.

0 radius

No rounded corners, no floating decoration. Elevation appears once, on the story artifact — the one object with depth.

Nodes are squares, rails are 1–2px rules, junctions are 14px. Branches carry their verdict as a labeled chip, not an arrowhead. Counts and dates sit beside every node so density stays honest.

Maps read left-to-right or top-to-bottom only — no radial graphs, no crossings. Anything a map says, an adjacent structured-text view says in the same order. The map is a rendering of the story, never the only copy of it.

Motion, where used, is a single 150ms ease on hover and focus. Under reduced motion, state changes swap instantly — nothing meaningful is animated-only.

09 · Why this supports trust, comprehension and learning
Trust

Claims are falsifiable by construction: every factual sentence can carry an evidence chip, inference is dashed before it is anything else, and the strongest visual state on the page belongs to human confirmation. The scope promises — read-only, private, deletable — are shown as interface, not asserted as paragraphs.

Comprehension

One story spine runs through the whole page — reported, weighed, reviewed, revised, merged, learned — and every artifact re-renders that same spine: strip, rail, map, prose. Plain language sits beside technical language, and every diagram has an equal structured-text form, so no single literacy is the price of entry.

Learning

The design treats verification as the learning mechanism: correcting a draft, answering “why did this change?”, teaching the fix back, and meeting the lesson again later are all retrieval practice. AI drafts; the person confirms — and the interface makes the confirming feel like the point, not a chore.
