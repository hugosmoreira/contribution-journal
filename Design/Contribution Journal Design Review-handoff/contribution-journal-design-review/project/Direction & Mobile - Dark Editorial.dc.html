01 · Creative direction

A reading room for engineering work — serif thought, mono evidence.

Two voices, one page

Newsreader carries the human layer — claims, quotes, lessons. IBM Plex Mono carries the machine layer — refs, counts, provenance. The reader always knows which one is speaking.

Deeply layered, never glowing

Four dark surfaces separated by hairlines and faint graph grids — depth from tone, not blur or bloom. Panels sit in the ground like plates on a desk.

Color is semantics only

Blue, green, violet, amber and red appear exclusively as states — evidence, confirmed, AI-drafted, review, rejected — each paired with a shape and a label so hue is never the only carrier.

Evidence

Blue #7DB1FF · solid border + link mark + ref. Ties a claim to its GitHub source. Also the interactive hue: links, focus ring, selection.

Confirmed

Green #5BD08B · solid border + check. Verified by a person, or by passing validation. The strongest state on the page.

AI draft

Violet #B294F5 · dashed border + asterisk. Drafted by AI, awaiting review — visually provisional by construction.

Inferred / review

Amber #E9B860 · dotted border. Reasoned without a direct source; also marks review feedback on timelines and quote bars.

Rejected

Red #F26D76 · solid border + cross. Abandoned approaches and destructive actions. Never used for emphasis.

Each state = hue + border style (solid / dashed / dotted) + mark + written label. Strip the color and the meaning survives. Tints stay under 12% opacity; the primary action is ink-on-dark, so semantic hues never compete with the CTA.

04 · Layering, spacing & timelines
1360px

Content column; sections at 96px rhythm, separated by 1px hairlines — no background jumps except the band surfaces.

4 layers

Ground → band → panel → well. Each step ≈3% lighter; borders do the separating, shadows appear once, under the story artifact.

72px grid

Faint graph lines (3% white) sit behind the hero and the close only — the product's coordinate system, not a texture.

6–12px radius

Soft, consistent corners; 150ms ease on hover/focus; under reduced motion, states swap instantly.

Nodes are 11px circles on 1px rails; the ringed blue node is reserved for the moment that needs a person. Branches carry their verdict as a labeled chip, never a bare arrowhead, and counts sit beside every node so density stays honest.

Maps read left-to-right or top-to-bottom only. Anything a map says, an adjacent structured-text view says in the same order — the map is a rendering of the story, never the only copy of it.

07 · Why this supports trust, comprehension and learning
Trust

Claims are falsifiable by construction: every factual sentence can carry a blue evidence chip, AI drafts are dashed violet before they are anything else, and green confirmation is the strongest state on the page. Read-only, private and deletable are shown as interface, not asserted as paragraphs.

Comprehension

One story spine runs through the whole page — reported, weighed, reviewed, revised, merged, learned — and every artifact re-renders it: strip, rail, map, prose. The serif/mono split separates human reasoning from machine record at a glance, and every diagram has an equal structured-text form.

Learning

Verification is the learning mechanism: correcting a draft, answering “why did this change?”, teaching the fix back, and meeting the lesson again later are all retrieval practice. AI drafts; the person confirms — and the ringed blue node makes that confirmation the visible center of the product.
